Autonomous Aquatic Waste-Collection Robot

2026

Developed an Arduino-based aquatic waste-collection robot with autonomous and remote-controlled modes, integrating colour and ultrasonic proximity sensors for 100% obstacle avoidance during testing, along with wireless operation and a battery-powered system providing one hour of runtime.

Arduino Robotics Autonomous Systems Aquatic Waste Collection Wireless Systems Battery Powered

Blue Quantum Dot LED

2026

Synthesized size-tunable CdSe quantum dots via kinetic growth, characterized their optical properties using UV–Vis and fluorescence spectroscopy, and fabricated a blue-emitting QLED stack with spin-coated PEDOT, TPD/PVK, CdSe, ZnO, ITO, and liquid gallium–tin alloy contacts.

Quantum Dots QLEDs Nanomaterials UV-Vis Spectroscopy Fluorescence Spectroscopy Spin Coating Thin Film Technology Optoelectronics

Silicon Nitride MIS Capacitors

2026

Fabricated and characterized SiNx MIS capacitors on p-type silicon using PECVD dielectric deposition, PVD-sputtered aluminum contacts, two-mask photolithography, alignment, RIE, wet etching, post-process inspection, and C–V/I–V measurements to evaluate dielectric quality, leakage behavior, and capacitor response.

Microfabrication Cleanroom PECVD PVD Photolithography RIE Wet etching

SPION Enhanced LCE

2025
