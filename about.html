About Me

Who Am I

My name is Owen Gibbs, and I am a 3rd year nanotechnology engineering student at the University of Waterloo from Edmonton, Alberta. I have professional experience in sensor developement and integration, microfabrication, advanced semiconductor packaging, and nanomaterial synthesis. I enjoy tackling complex challenges and using creative thinking to develop innovative solutions.

  • Material synthesis and characterization
  • Sensor design and experimental testing
  • Computational Modelling and Simulation
  • Rapid Prototyping (CAD, 3D Printing, CNC, Laser Cutting, Welding)
  • Machine Learning

Interests
